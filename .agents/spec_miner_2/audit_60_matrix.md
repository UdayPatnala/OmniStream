## Master Authority Hierarchy
1. **PRODUCT_MOTIVE** (Simplicity, reliability, personal convenience, theatrical presentation)
2. **USER_SAFETY & SECURITY** (Zero XSS, CSP enforcement, DRM/provider compliance, zero secret leakage)
3. **DATA_INTEGRITY & PRIVACY** (Zero backend, air-gapped local persistence, zero data loss, corruption recovery)
4. **CORE_PLAYBACK** (Playback stability takes absolute priority over AI/effects; zero blocking of video decoding)
5. **PERFORMANCE** (Sub-2s initial load, 60fps rendering, zero memory leaks, graceful resource throttling)
6. **UX & ACCESSIBILITY** (Keyboard accessibility, ARIA compliance, 1-click resume, responsive design)
7. **ADVANCED_FEATURES** (Client-side ML framing, procedural Web Audio synthesis, 3D theater proscenium)
8. **DECORATIVE_EFFECTS** (Ticket printer animation, ambilight glow, vintage paper accents)
